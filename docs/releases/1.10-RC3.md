# NeonPocketMC Suite v1.10.0 RC3

The suite now pins ULP Solar Repeaters v1.0.0-rc.4 at
`c12d218cf956ccca0186cc1e91f628f4857786de`, with all twenty firmware files and the
corrected USB configurator verified against their published SHA-256 digests.

The configurator asserts DTR for nRF52 USB CDC replies and waits for complete
reply lines. It includes macOS guidance and works with existing rc.2/rc.3
firmware without a reflash or settings reset. Physical validation on the
reported Apple Silicon Mac/RAK combination remains outstanding.

Other product releases and their artifact pins are preserved. Use the exact
hardware/profile selection in the flasher; ULP remains an experimental candidate.
